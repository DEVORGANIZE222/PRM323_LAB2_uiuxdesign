# UX Research: User Persona & Problem Statement

## 1. Primary Persona

| Attribute | Details |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Nguyen Van Minh |
| **Age** | 20 years old |
| **Role** | 7th Semester Software Engineering Student, FPT University |
| **Daily Context** | Attends lectures on campus 4 days a week, does part-time backend development 15 hours a week, and communicates primarily through Telegram and mobile notifications. Highly constrained for time during the semester registration week. |
| **Device Habits** | Primary device is an Android smartphone (Xiaomi 13, 6.36-inch, 1080x2400 resolution, viewport ~392dp). Uses phone on-the-go (bus, campus cafeteria) using one-handed thumb interaction. |
| **Accessibility Needs** | Mild astigmatism; struggles with low-contrast gray text on white backgrounds under direct campus sunlight. Requires minimum 48x48dp touch targets because of quick one-handed interactions. |

### Goals
- Discover Capstone groups that are still actively looking for backend/AI engineering roles before the hard registration deadline.
- Quickly inspect who is currently inside a group (skills, experience, contact).
- Participate in team leadership voting seamlessly.
- Know with 100% certainty when the team is ready and legally compliant (4–5 members) to be locked.

### Pain Points
- **Unclear availability:** Existing spreadsheet/chat methods cause students to request groups that are already full or abandoned.
- **Deadlock on Leader assignment:** Teams don't know who has the authority to submit and lock the final group roster.
- **Risk of invalid rosters:** Accidental locking or deadline passing with only 3 members results in groups getting forcibly randomized by administration.
- **Notification clutter:** Misses critical group invitation acceptance status amidst chaotic group chat feeds.

---

## 2. One-Sentence Problem Statement
> *"Capstone students struggle to find compatible, available teammates, track group capacity (4–5 members), and coordinate leader assignment before the strict semester registration deadline."*

---

## 3. Measurable Success Criterion
> **Criterion:** A student can discover and join an eligible group with open slots in under **60 seconds**, and a team leader can verify roster compliance and execute the group lock action in under **15 seconds** without encountering unexpected validation errors.
